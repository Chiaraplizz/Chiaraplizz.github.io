Hello! I’m Chiara and I am an Assistant Professor at Bocconi University, in the [Department of Computing Sciences](https://cs.unibocconi.eu/), where I lead the **Vision and Video Understanding** group. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Smart Eyewear Lab](https://www.essilorluxottica.com/it/careers/smart-eyewear-lab/), a joint research center between EssilorLuxottica and Politecnico di Milano, in the group of Prof. [Matteo Matteucci](https://www.deib.polimi.it/eng/people/details/267262). Prior to that, I was a Student Researcher at [Google Research](https://research.google/people/federicotombari/?&type=google) in Zurich, where I worked on multi-modal LLMs for video understanding in [Federico Tombari](https://federicotombari.github.io/)'s team. I did my PhD in the [VANDAL](http://vandal.polito.it/) (Visual AND Multimodal Applied Learning) laboratory at [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it/), supervised by Prof. [Barbara Caputo](https://www.polito.it/en/staff?p=barbara.caputo), as an [ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/) PhD student co-supervised by Prof. [Dima Damen](https://dimadamen.github.io/) (University of Bristol). During my PhD I visited the University of Bristol and the Berkeley AI Research Lab (BAIR), collaborating with Prof. [Angjoo Kanazawa](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~kanazawa/).

I am interested in **video understanding** and **egocentric vision**, and intrigued by how **vision** can be complemented by **multiple modalities**, including language, audio, and 3D information, towards **4D scene understanding**.
