Hello! I’m Chiara and I'm a Postdoctoral Research at the [Smart Eyewear Lab]([http://vandal.polito.it/](https://www.essilorluxottica.com/it/careers/smart-eyewear-lab/)), a joint research lab between EssilorLuxottica and Politecnico di Milano. Previously, I have been a PhD student of the [VANDAL](http://vandal.polito.it/) (Visual AND Multimodal Applied Learning) laboratory at [Politecnico di Torino (Italy)](https://www.polito.it/), supervised by prof. [Barbara Caputo](https://www.polito.it/en/staff?p=barbara.caputo). I was also part of the [ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/programs) research program co-supervised by Prof. [Dima Damen](http://people.cs.bris.ac.uk/~damen//). I am interested in Video Understanding, and intrigued by how Vision can be complemented by multiple modalities, including Language, Audio, and 3D information.
