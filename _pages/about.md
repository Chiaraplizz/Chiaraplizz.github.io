Hello! I’m Chiara and I am currently an Assistant Professor at Bocconi University, in the [Department of Computing Sciences](https://cs.unibocconi.eu/). Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Smart Eyewear Lab](https://www.essilorluxottica.com/it/careers/smart-eyewear-lab/), a joint research center between EssilorLuxottica and Politecnico di Milano. Prior to that, I was a Student Researcher at [Google Research](https://research.google/people/federicotombari/?&type=google) in Zurich, where I had the chance to work in [Federico Tombari](https://federicotombari.github.io/)'s team. I have been a PhD student of the [VANDAL](http://vandal.polito.it/) (Visual AND Multimodal Applied Learning) laboratory at [Politecnico di Torino (Italy)](https://www.polito.it/), supervised by prof. [Barbara Caputo](https://www.polito.it/en/staff?p=barbara.caputo). I was also part of the [ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/programs) research program co-supervised by Prof. [Dima Damen](http://people.cs.bris.ac.uk/~damen//) from University of Bristol. 

I am interested in <strong>video understanding</strong>, and intrigued by how <strong>vision</strong> can be complemented by <strong>multiple modalities</strong>, including language, audio, and 3D information.
